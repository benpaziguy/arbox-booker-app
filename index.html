Arbox booker

How it works

Sign in with your gym (Arbox) email and password — the same login as the gym's own app. That's the only setup. The app books your classes for you, including the ones that fill the instant registration opens (usually 48h before; 72h for Sun/Mon) — even at 3am, whether or not your phone is on.

The buttons on a class
Weekly bookings

Add to weekly books that class every week automatically. Stop recurring ends it. Going to miss just one week? Tap Skip on that date — it pauses only that week. Tap Queue (top) to see Your weekly classes and the status of each: booked, books at its window, skipped, or paused.

Good to know
  • The gym allows one booking per class type per day. A second is refused — the app tells you. A waiting-list spot is still allowed.
  • Cancelling a class your weekly rule books also skips that date, so it isn't instantly re-booked. It resumes the next week.
  • Manage your account under Queue → Account (sign out, or delete your saved schedule and stored login).
  • Tap ⋯ on a class for more: WOD (that day's workout), Who's in (who's booked), and the weekly controls. A ⭐ on a card means an Arbox friend is going.
  • Use the chips above the days to filter by type — W.O.D, HYROX, Open Gym, and so on.
  • Hit Feedback (top) to send a bug report or feature request straight to the app's maintainer.

This page talks straight to Arbox from your browser — the same API the gym's own site uses. Nothing goes through any other server. Your password is used once to get a session token and is never stored; the token lives in this tab only and is gone when you close it.

Registration opens 48h before most classes, 72h for Sunday and Monday. A class further out shows Queue: tap it and GitHub Actions claims the seat the second its window opens, days later, with nothing of yours running. Your recurring weekly slots are already handled the same way.

Queued classes

Queueing and weekly bookings are set up automatically when you sign in — the same email and password. Nothing else to configure on this phone.

Notifications

Account

Sign out just clears this phone. Delete account removes your saved schedule and stored gym credentials from the service for good — it cannot be undone.
