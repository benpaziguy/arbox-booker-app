Arbox booker

This page talks straight to Arbox from your browser — the same API the gym's own site uses. Nothing goes through any other server. Your password is used once to get a session token and is never stored; the token lives in this tab only and is gone when you close it.

Registration opens 48h before most classes, 72h for Sunday and Monday. A class further out shows Queue: tap it and GitHub Actions claims the seat the second its window opens, days later, with nothing of yours running. Your recurring weekly slots are already handled the same way.

Queued classes

Queueing and weekly bookings are set up automatically when you sign in — the same email and password. Nothing else to configure on this phone.
